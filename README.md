# Walmart Sales Performance Analysis 📊

### Project Overview
This project consists of a detailed analysis of the commercial performance of Walmart Mexico and Central America, processing transactional data to identify sales patterns and operational efficiencies. The primary objective was to transform massive datasets into key indicators for strategic decision-making.

### Technical Workflow
The analysis was developed through a structured multi-stage workflow:

* **Data Processing (ETL):** Multiple raw data sources were consolidated, including sales records, store information, and department categories.
* **Cleaning & Standardization:** A deep cleaning process was performed in the `ventas_clean` sheet, where date formats were standardized and record integrity was ensured for subsequent analysis.
* **Data Modeling:** Pivot tables (`Pivot`) were used to aggregate and segment sales volume by time periods and business units.
* **Visualization (Dashboard):** An interactive dashboard was designed to centralize the main KPIs, allowing for a rapid interpretation of commercial performance.

### Key Findings
The following quantitative results were obtained through the analysis:

* **Total Sales Volume:** A total of **$4,001,475,414.00** in sales was processed.
* **Top Performing Stores:** The stores with the highest sales volume were **Store 20, Store 4, and Store 14**.
* **Leading Departments:** The best-performing departments were **92, 95, and 38**.
* **Seasonality Impact:** Sales behavior was analyzed during critical periods such as the Super Bowl, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas, identifying specific demand peaks by category.

### Repository Content
1.  **Raw Data:** Base files containing sales information and catalogs.
2.  **Processed Data:** Clean sales record ready for analysis (`ventas_clean`).
3.  **Analysis:** Pivot tables and executive summaries (`Pivot`, `Resumen`).
4.  **Dashboard:** Interactive visual panel for results presentation.
